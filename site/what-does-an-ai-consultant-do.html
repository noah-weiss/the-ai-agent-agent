What does an AI consultant actually do?

An AI consultant audits how you actually work, maps where AI saves real time, builds the workflows worth building, and keeps you from buying tools you don't need. The good ones are accountable for results, not reports.

The work, concretely

  • Audit: what's working, what's noise, what's costing you time
  • Workflow map: which workflows matter this week versus later
  • Prioritized plan: what to do next, in order, and what to ignore
  • Builds: agents and automations that remove real grunt work
  • Vendor filter: someone pitches you AI software, you forward it before you reply

When to hire one instead of buying another tool

When you don't know which tool matters, the problem isn't the tool. It's the map. Buying software before mapping your workflows is how firms end up paying for five subscriptions nobody opens.

What to watch out for

AI theater: decks about transformation, frameworks about adoption, pilots that never touch a real workflow. If an engagement doesn't name the specific workflow it will change, it's theater.
