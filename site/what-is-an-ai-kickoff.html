What is an AI Kickoff?

A 60 to 90 minute live mapping and strategy session. I audit where you actually are with AI, map the workflows that matter, and you leave with a prioritized plan and a comprehensive recap the same day.

What's in it

  • Workflow audit: what's working, what's noise, what's costing you time
  • Opportunity map: which workflows matter this week versus later
  • Prioritized action plan: next moves in order, and what to ignore
  • Comprehensive recap: written, same day, so nothing evaporates

What it is not

It's not a course, and it's not the full concierge relationship. Multi-week builds, company rollouts, and on-call support are separate. The Kickoff is the map; it stands alone either way.

What happens after

Builds come when the map says go: scoped agents and automations that remove real grunt work. If you want one person accountable long term, that's the concierge relationship.

Format

Zoom or in person in Los Angeles. Screen share, hands-on where it serves the map. My clients have spanned beauty, insurance, law, and finance (and grandmas).
