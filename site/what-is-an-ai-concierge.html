What is an AI concierge?

An AI concierge is one person accountable for AI working in your business. Instead of you testing every tool and watching half a tutorial, your concierge picks what is worth using, builds the workflows that matter, and stays in your corner as things change.

What the job actually covers

  • Deciding which AI platforms are worth your time and which are noise
  • Building or setting up agents and workflows when something should exist
  • Evaluating tools and vendors before you spend money on them
  • Troubleshooting and decision support when something breaks or changes
  • Protecting you from AI theater and bad purchases

How it differs from a course

A course teaches you to do the work yourself, on your own time, with no one checking whether anything shipped. A concierge does the work with you and stays accountable for the result. A new tool drops, you send one message. Somebody pitches you AI software, you forward it to your concierge before you reply.

Who uses one

Founders, operators, and professionals who know AI matters but don't have time to test every tool, map every workflow, or manage every implementation. My clients have spanned beauty, insurance, law, and finance.

How it starts

It starts with an AI Kickoff: a mapping and strategy session that audits where you are and gives you a prioritized plan. If you want me in your corner after the map, that's the concierge relationship.
